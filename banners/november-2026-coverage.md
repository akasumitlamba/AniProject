# November 2026 banner coverage

The 178-entry baseline had 173 entries with Android packages and 108 with website hosts. These counts overlap. This update adds 100 previously unlisted Android app packages and 100 previously unlisted website entries for pinned shortcuts. Some of the new Android banners also cover their web counterparts. The catalog now contains 378 banners.

Selection balances India and global popularity with services people can use from a tablet or pin in a browser. The lists are alphabetical and do not claim to be exact download or traffic rankings. Android identities were checked against Google Play. Website rows use the host shown in the table. Where an existing or new app banner covers the same service, its additional verified hosts share that banner.

Ranking references are [Similarweb Android apps in India](https://www.similarweb.com/top-apps/google/india/top-free/), [global site traffic](https://www.similarweb.com/top-websites/), and [site traffic in India](https://www.similarweb.com/top-websites/india/). The Android list includes globally popular apps whose availability varies by region.
