# AniProject 1.1.0

A big update to AniHome: a new Control Center with more controls than Android's own and your apps' quick actions, a separate settings app, AniHome's own volume panel with per-app sound control, a weather widget, smarter Bluetooth and Wi-Fi controls, and a polished look throughout. AniBrave moves to a newer Brave engine.

**Updated 15 November 2026:** AniHome now has its own **gesture navigation** with a thin handle and no navigation bar, replacing the separate Edge Navigation app. The volume panel is redesigned, banners load faster and the banner store is more reliable, and crashes when closing the swipe-down panel or using **Clear all** are fixed. AniBrave's vertical tabs can switch and close tabs again. Install the new AniHome and AniBrave over the old ones; the extension and banners are unchanged.

**Updated 17 November 2026:** AniHome's settings, banner screens and wallpaper picker get a Material You redesign, and AniHome Settings has its own icon. The wallpaper picker shows each whole picture and frames portrait and landscape at once on the same image. New options: show apps as a list, hide the dock's time, date, battery or messages indicator, a sliding dock message of your own, and Android's status bar on Home; the dock search button is now off by default. Fixes: an app switched from icon to banner gets its banner, the panel's controls expand and collapse with swipes and start collapsed each time, a pull from the top no longer brings Android's shade over AniHome's panel, and the first-launch setup stays until both permissions are given.

**Updated 16 November 2026:** a **security fix**: websites and other apps can no longer open a page in AniBrave's fullscreen shortcut window (with no address bar), so a fake page can't pose as one of your shortcuts. AniHome adds a **wallpaper picker** with AniHome's own collections, gallery and other apps, where you frame portrait and landscape separately at your screen's exact shape. Also new: an option to use Android's own notification panel and status bar, banners that load straight from the online store (the app is now 3 MB instead of 25 MB), a readable notification panel that closes with a swipe up, a steadier dock with apps centred on it, banners that grow and fade as Home scrolls, reorganised settings, a choice to add apps as banners or icons, and a short first-launch setup. AniBrave's **new-tab homepage** (clock, search with your default engine, weather) now opens properly instead of showing "This site can't be reached". Install both apps over the old ones.

## AniHome

### Control Center

Open notifications from the dock clock, or swipe down from the top of the screen in **any app**.

- **One-tap controls**: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, auto-rotate, flashlight, Do not disturb, location, dark theme, night light, sound mode (ring, vibrate, silent), battery saver, airplane mode, mobile data, hotspot, stay awake, auto brightness, invert colours, cast, screenshot, lock and settings.
- **App actions**: add the quick actions your apps offer (like a new message, a new note or a camera mode) as one-tap controls. Tap the pencil, then **Add an app action**.
- Night light, invert colours and mobile data switch with one tap when Shizuku is set up; without it they open the right Settings page.
- **Fold or expand** the controls by swiping up or down on them, or with the arrow.
- **Edit** (pencil) chooses which controls appear and in what order.
- **Brightness and volume** sliders, and a **now playing** card with play/pause and skip.
- **Wi-Fi and Bluetooth** open as full-height lists you can scroll, with a back arrow.
- **Two layouts**: a side panel, or full screen with tall brightness and volume sliders and notifications in two even columns. Phones always use the full screen.
- **Blurred glass or solid** background. Over your Home screen the glass blurs your wallpaper.
- **Clear all** clears everything in one sweep. Notifications that Android won't let anyone dismiss (like USB or charging) stay in the list but go quiet: they move to the bottom, dim, and no longer count toward the badge.
- Long-press a notification for **Settings**, **Snooze for 1 hour** or **Dismiss**.
- Turning airplane mode off puts Wi-Fi and Bluetooth back the way they were.

### Gesture navigation

Choose it in **AniHome Settings → Navigation**. Android's navigation bar disappears and a thin handle sits on the bottom edge, so apps and your Home screen use the whole screen.

- **Swipe up** to go Home. The app shrinks into Home under your finger.
- **Swipe up and hold** for recent apps. From Home, any swipe up opens them.
- **Drag sideways** along the bottom to switch between your recent apps. They keep their places, so going right and then left brings you back to the same app.
- **Double-tap** the bottom edge to go back.
- The handle always shows on Home. In apps it appears when you touch the bottom edge and fades out after a couple of seconds.
- When Auto-rotate is off in Android's quick settings, the screen stays at the angle it was in (portrait or either landscape) as you move between apps and Home.
- **Android buttons** brings back the standard navigation bar at any time.

Setting it up the first time needs Shizuku. The moving app animations and app switching also use Shizuku; without it, Home, Back and Recents still work. Gesture navigation is available on the TCL 9080G tablet with Android 10; other devices keep Android's own navigation. If you used the separate Edge Navigation app, AniHome turns it off for you, and you can uninstall it.

### AniHome volume panel

Turn it on in **AniHome Settings → Sound & Bluetooth**. The volume buttons then show a compact panel on the right edge, centred top to bottom in every orientation:

- a thin volume line for media, in the style of Android's own panel. Hold a volume button to keep changing it;
- the arrow adds lines for ring, alerts and alarm, plus **every app that's playing sound** (needs Shizuku). Tap a sound's icon to mute or unmute it;
- where Android allows it, each app gets its own volume line, and your levels come back when the app plays again. On Android 10, Android only lets AniHome **mute or unmute** apps, so tapping the app's icon does that. Apps you mute stay listed until you unmute them.

The panel closes when you tap outside it, or by itself after a few seconds. Calls and the lock screen keep Android's own volume panel.

### Bluetooth and Wi-Fi

- Devices show an **icon for what they are**: headphones, earbuds, speakers, laptops, phones, watches, keyboards, mice, gamepads, cars and TVs.
- Long-press a device, or tap **⋯**, for **Connect/Disconnect**, **Forget**, and **Auto-connect**. Turn auto-connect off and that device only connects when you tap it.
- Saved Wi-Fi networks have the same menu with **Auto-connect** (Android 11 and newer).
- **Keep two Bluetooth audio devices connected** and switch between them instantly (needs Shizuku). Android plays sound on one device at a time unless your phone has its own dual-audio feature.

### AniHome Settings

AniHome Settings is now **its own app** with its own icon in your app list. It holds every launcher setting in one place: rotation, dock, app menu, banners, notification panel, swipe-down panel, sound and Bluetooth, gestures, Home layout and names.

- **Search** to find any setting.
- On tablets the sections are listed on the left and their options on the right, each scrolling on its own.
- Changes to the panel show live while you adjust them.

### Home screen

- **Hold** an item to see its options. **Hold and move** to pick it up and rearrange; editing begins when you let go.
- Options are now a **compact row of icons** (Rename, Image, Icon/Banner, Dock, Info, Uninstall, Remove) plus the app's own shortcuts, so there's no scrolling.
- **Long-press empty space** for a Home menu: add widgets, apps or websites, change the **wallpaper**, open settings or lock the layout.
- Items lift when picked up and glide into place when dropped.
- Rotating a tablet no longer leaves an empty column; portrait and landscape each keep their own arrangement.
- **Weather widget**: current temperature, conditions and today's high and low. Choose your city once; no location permission or account needed.
- Store banners now appear automatically for **websites** and **app shortcuts** too, not only apps.
- **Websites find their banner without per-site rules**: AniHome breaks a site's address and page title into words and matches them against the store, so a site and its app always share one banner (JioHotstar's website gets the same banner as its app). When two banners would fit equally, the icon stays rather than guessing. Website tiles that showed only an icon are checked again once.
- Banners are **ready before you add an app**: when you install an app, AniHome downloads its store banner straight away, so it shows the moment you add the app to Home. Only banners for apps you have installed are downloaded, and a banner is removed when its app is uninstalled.
- While a banner is still loading, the tile shows a small spinner. If the store doesn't have a banner for an app, AniHome tells you and keeps the app's icon.
- The **banner store** loads faster and more reliably with large collections: several banners download at once, a banner that fails no longer stops the rest from loading, recently updated banners always arrive in their new version, and a single faulty entry in the collection can't hide the others.
- New installs start with **AniBrave, YouTube and Google**. If AniBrave isn't installed, its tile offers to download and install it directly.

### Dock

- A new **clock block**: the time, the date, and a battery icon with the percentage. The battery turns green with a lightning bolt while charging and red when low. It fits neatly in both the bottom and side docks.
- Choose between **pinned apps** or **recently used apps** (long-press the dock).
- App icons have smooth, clean edges.

### App menu

- **Recent apps** is a single row that matches the grid, with no sideways scrolling, sitting just above All applications.
- **Recent files** shows real previews for photos, videos and APKs, and clean type icons for documents.
- Turn **Recent apps** and **Recent files** on or off.
- Pick an **icon shape** by seeing the shapes themselves.

### Shizuku made easy

Shizuku is optional. **AniHome Settings → Shizuku** has a step-by-step guide: install it, start it, then allow AniHome. Each step ticks itself off when you come back. Without Shizuku, controls open Android's own settings instead, with at most a small reminder, never a pop-up in your way.

## AniBrave for Android

- **Vertical tabs work again**: tapping a tab switches to it and ✕ closes it (with Undo), instead of showing "Tab action unavailable".
- The horizontal tab strip no longer pops up when something briefly covers the browser (a permission or sign-in sheet, the share menu, AniHome's swipe-down panel or split screen).
- The **AniBrave features** button (playback speed, site view, fullscreen shortcut, watched anime, streaming buffer, tab layout) now sits at the top right, next to the ⋮ menu, with vertical tabs too.
- The tab rail's expand/collapse button moved to the bottom, next to New tab and the tab overview.
- Fullscreen website shortcuts now tell the launcher which site they open, so AniHome can show the site's banner.
- **A new homepage** for new tabs: a large clock and date, a search box that uses your default search engine, and the weather for a city you choose (from Open-Meteo, no account). It's built in, loads instantly and works offline (weather needs a connection).
- **Smoother on busy sites**: AniBrave's playback helpers now handle page changes in small batches instead of re-scanning large parts of the page on every change, and the vertical tab rail does far less work per frame.

- Updated to **Brave 1.99.57 (Chromium 157)** for the latest browser fixes.
- Desktop and mobile site views now identify as the current Chrome version, so sites don't treat the browser as outdated.
- Fewer false "page controls unavailable" messages while pages load.
- In fullscreen website shortcuts, the first swipe from the screen edge now goes back as expected.

## AniBrave desktop extension

- The mobile site view now matches your browser's real Chrome version instead of an older one.

To update, replace the files in the extension's folder, press **Reload** on its card in `brave://extensions`, then reload your open site tabs.

## Banners

Banners are not part of the release downloads. AniHome gets them from the online banner store, which is updated separately from the apps.

The collection has grown to **402 banners** for popular apps and websites, India and worldwide, including **AniHome Settings**, and 24 existing banners have fresh artwork. AniHome picks up new banners from the online store automatically, with no app update needed.

## Permissions and privacy

New optional permissions are used only for the features you turn on:

- **Accessibility (AniHome controls)** now also powers the swipe-down panel and the AniHome volume panel. It reacts only to the volume keys and never reads what's on screen.
- **Shizuku**, if you set it up, enables per-app volume, one-tap system toggles, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi extras.
- **App installs** are only requested when you choose to install AniBrave or an update.
- **Weather** sends only the city you picked to Open-Meteo.

## Things to know

- Some phone makers lock dark theme to their own Settings. On those devices the dark theme control hides itself.
- Per-app volume depends on how each app plays sound; a few apps reset their own volume.
- Wi-Fi auto-connect per network needs Android 11 or newer.
- Bluetooth sound plays on one device at a time unless your phone supports dual audio.
