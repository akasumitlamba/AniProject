# AniProject 1.1.0

A big update to AniHome: a new Control Center with more controls than Android's own and your apps' quick actions, a separate settings app, AniHome's own volume panel with per-app volume, a weather widget, smarter Bluetooth and Wi-Fi controls, and a polished look throughout. AniBrave moves to a newer Brave engine.

## AniHome

### Control Center

Open notifications from the dock clock, or swipe down from the top of the screen in **any app**.

- **One-tap controls**: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, auto-rotate, flashlight, Do not disturb, location, dark theme, night light, sound mode (ring, vibrate, silent), battery saver, airplane mode, mobile data, hotspot, stay awake, auto brightness, invert colours, cast, screenshot, lock and settings.
- **App actions**: add the quick actions your apps offer (like a new message, a new note or a camera mode) as one-tap controls. Tap the pencil, then **Add an app action**.
- Night light, invert colours and mobile data switch with one tap when Shizuku is set up; without it they open the right Settings page.
- **Fold or expand** the controls by swiping up or down on them, or with the arrow.
- **Edit** (pencil) chooses which controls appear and in what order.
- **Brightness and volume** sliders, and a **now playing** card with play/pause and skip.
- **Wi-Fi and Bluetooth** open as full-height lists you can scroll, with a back arrow.
- **Two layouts**: a side panel, or full screen with tall brightness and volume sliders and notifications in two even columns. Phones always use the full screen.
- **Blurred glass or solid** background. Over your Home screen the glass blurs your wallpaper.
- Notifications slide away smoothly, and **Clear all** clears them in one sweep. Ongoing notifications that Android keeps (like USB or charging) explain why they can't be dismissed.
- Long-press a notification for **Settings**, **Snooze for 1 hour** or **Dismiss**.
- Turning airplane mode off puts Wi-Fi and Bluetooth back the way they were.

### AniHome volume panel

Turn it on in **AniHome Settings → Sound & Bluetooth**. The volume buttons then show AniHome's own panel on the right edge. It includes:

- media volume, with ring, notifications and alarm a tap away;
- **a slider for every app that's playing sound**, so you can turn one app down without touching the others (needs Shizuku);
- your per-app levels are remembered and come back when the app plays again.

Calls and the lock screen keep Android's own volume panel.

### Bluetooth and Wi-Fi

- Devices show an **icon for what they are**: headphones, earbuds, speakers, laptops, phones, watches, keyboards, mice, gamepads, cars and TVs.
- Long-press a device, or tap **⋯**, for **Connect/Disconnect**, **Forget**, and **Auto-connect**. Turn auto-connect off and that device only connects when you tap it.
- Saved Wi-Fi networks have the same menu with **Auto-connect** (Android 11 and newer).
- **Keep two Bluetooth audio devices connected** and switch between them instantly (needs Shizuku). Android plays sound on one device at a time unless your phone has its own dual-audio feature.

### AniHome Settings

AniHome Settings is now **its own app** with its own icon in your app list. It holds every launcher setting in one place: rotation, dock, app menu, banners, notification panel, swipe-down panel, sound and Bluetooth, gestures, Home layout and names.

- **Search** to find any setting.
- On tablets the sections are listed on the left and their options on the right, each scrolling on its own.
- Changes to the panel show live while you adjust them.

### Home screen

- **Hold** an item to see its options. **Hold and move** to pick it up and rearrange; editing begins when you let go.
- Options are now a **compact row of icons** (Rename, Image, Icon/Banner, Dock, Info, Uninstall, Remove) plus the app's own shortcuts, so there's no scrolling.
- **Long-press empty space** for a Home menu: add widgets, apps or websites, change the **wallpaper**, open settings or lock the layout.
- Items lift when picked up and glide into place when dropped.
- Rotating a tablet no longer leaves an empty column; portrait and landscape each keep their own arrangement.
- **Weather widget**: current temperature, conditions and today's high and low. Choose your city once; no location permission or account needed.
- Store banners now appear automatically for **websites** and **app shortcuts** too, not only apps.
- New installs start with **AniBrave, YouTube and Google**. If AniBrave isn't installed, its tile offers to download and install it directly.

### Dock

- A new **clock block**: the time, the date, and a slim battery bar with the percentage. It turns green while charging and red when low, and works in both the bottom and side docks.
- Choose between **pinned apps** or **recently used apps** (long-press the dock).
- App icons have smooth, clean edges.

### App menu

- **Recent apps** is a single row that matches the grid, with no sideways scrolling.
- **Recent files** shows real previews for photos, videos and APKs, and clean type icons for documents.
- Turn **Recent apps** and **Recent files** on or off.
- Pick an **icon shape** by seeing the shapes themselves.

### Shizuku made easy

Features that need Shizuku explain why, and walk you through the one missing step: getting Shizuku, starting it, or allowing AniHome. Everything else works without it.

## AniBrave for Android

- Updated to **Brave 1.99.57 (Chromium 157)** for the latest browser fixes.
- Desktop and mobile site views now identify as the current Chrome version, so sites don't treat the browser as outdated.
- Fewer false "page controls unavailable" messages while pages load.
- In fullscreen website shortcuts, the first swipe from the screen edge now goes back as expected.

## AniBrave desktop extension

- The mobile site view now matches your browser's real Chrome version instead of an older one.

To update, replace the files in the extension's folder, press **Reload** on its card in `brave://extensions`, then reload your open site tabs.

## Banners

The collection now includes a banner for **AniHome Settings**: **178 app banners** in total.

## Permissions and privacy

New optional permissions are used only for the features you turn on:

- **Accessibility (AniHome controls)** now also powers the swipe-down panel and the AniHome volume panel. It reacts only to the volume keys and never reads what's on screen.
- **Shizuku**, if you set it up, enables per-app volume, one-tap system toggles, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi extras.
- **App installs** are only requested when you choose to install AniBrave or an update.
- **Weather** sends only the city you picked to Open-Meteo.

## Things to know

- Some phone makers lock dark theme to their own Settings. On those devices the dark theme control hides itself.
- Per-app volume depends on how each app plays sound; a few apps reset their own volume.
- Wi-Fi auto-connect per network needs Android 11 or newer.
- Bluetooth sound plays on one device at a time unless your phone supports dual audio.
