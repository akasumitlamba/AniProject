# AniProject 1.0.0

AniHome gives your Android home screen a flexible layout for apps, banners and widgets. AniBrave adds playback controls, site views and episode tracking to browsing, with a companion extension for Brave on desktop.

## AniHome

### Your home screen, your layout

Arrange applications, website shortcuts and Android widgets together. Keep a wallpaper-only home screen if you prefer, or build a layout with banners, compact app icons and useful widgets.

- Add an app from the application menu to create a home tile.
- Choose a banner from the catalog, select your own image, or switch the tile to an app icon.
- When a matching banner is available, a newly added app can fetch it automatically. Otherwise, it uses the app icon. Your chosen artwork takes priority over automatic suggestions.
- Show, hide or rename tile labels.
- Hold an item to move it, or keep holding to open its options. Hold an empty area to enter edit mode; tap an empty area or finish editing to leave it.
- Resize widgets from their edges and move them from the center. Removing an item frees space for the remaining layout, and Undo lets you recover an accidental removal.
- Lock the layout when you want to prevent accidental changes.

Removing a tile from home leaves the application installed.

### Widgets

Use Android widgets alongside AniHome’s clock and date, battery and quick-note widgets. Quick notes keep a local draft so you can return to unfinished text. Widget sizes and arrangements adapt to the available screen space.

This release improves tile visibility, dragging, removal controls and layout compaction, including homes with enough items to scroll.

### Applications and recent files

The application menu brings favorites, installed apps and recent files together. Newly installed applications are discovered without needing to restart the launcher.

On larger screens, recent files appear beside the application grid, with favorites above the full app list. On phones, the layout adapts to the narrower screen and the recent-files area can expand for easier access. Search helps you find apps and files, with a visible browser-search action for web queries.

Application options include adding to home, hiding, viewing Android app information and uninstalling. Hidden apps can be restored. Recent files support opening, sharing and viewing information; availability depends on Android’s file-access permissions.

### Dock

The dock provides the time and date, application menu, browser/search shortcut and room for pinned apps. Optional controls include Recents and notifications. In landscape, the dock stays at the side; portrait uses a layout suited to the available width.

- Choose the browser used by the search shortcut, or hide that shortcut.
- Show or hide the Recents button independently of gesture actions.
- See battery percentage, a charging indicator while charging, and a USB indicator when connected. The battery icon changes from red to yellow to green as the charge increases.
- Use wallpaper-based dynamic opacity, or set the dock background opacity yourself. The dock outline remains visible so its boundaries stay clear.

### Notifications and quick settings

Open the notification sidebar through its dock control or an assigned gesture. Tap outside the sidebar to dismiss it.

Notifications support expansion, available notification actions, dismissal, Clear all and a one-hour snooze. You can also open an app’s Android notification settings. Android may prevent some ongoing or system notifications from being dismissed.

Wi-Fi and Bluetooth expand inside the sidebar, with power controls, refresh and scrollable device or network lists. Connected and recently used entries are easier to find. Radio controls depend on the permissions and capabilities Android exposes; optional Shizuku access enables additional controls on supported devices. Android may still require a system confirmation for pairing, permissions or restricted settings.

### Gestures and motion

Assign actions separately to swipes up, down, left and right. Double-tapping an empty area can lock the screen when the required Accessibility access is enabled. Scrolling a populated home takes priority over navigation gestures.

Panels slide in from the direction of the assigned swipe. Drawer transitions, notification expansion and dismissal use short animations, with reduced-motion support.

### Rotation

Use **Auto** to follow device rotation, then choose **Fixed** to keep the current orientation. Changing system rotation requires Android’s system-settings permission. Some applications and device firmware impose their own orientation restrictions.

A system rotation lock can remain enabled after uninstalling a launcher. To change it afterward, use Android’s Auto-rotate control.

## AniBrave for Android

AniBrave is a separate browser based on Brave. You can keep it installed alongside the official Brave browser; each has its own browsing profile.

### Playback controls

Open AniBrave’s feature controls beside the tabs to adjust playback speed, site view, fullscreen shortcuts, episode tracking and streaming-buffer preferences.

Playback speed choices range from **0.5× to 4×**. The browser’s speed setting and a supported website’s own speed control stay in step. Your selected speed is remembered for the site and reapplied when its player changes. A lower limit in a website’s menu does not necessarily limit the speed AniBrave can request, although protected or restricted players may reject changes.

### Site views and tabs

Choose a mobile view, a desktop identity with a mobile layout, or a desktop view. Preferences are remembered per site; a reload may be needed for a website to serve the selected layout.

Use horizontal tabs or a vertical tab sidebar. Regular and private tabs remain distinguishable, with compact rows and tab counts to help manage longer browsing sessions.

### Fullscreen website shortcuts

Add a fullscreen shortcut to your Android home screen through the shortcut confirmation. AniHome supports these pinned shortcuts. They open with browser controls hidden for a more app-like view, while keeping access to the browser’s tools and the website’s existing signed-in session.

Fullscreen shortcuts support Android Recents. Website availability and functionality still depend on the site and your connection.

### Streaming buffer

Choose the site’s default buffering behavior or request a **1-, 3-, 5- or 10-minute** buffer. An optional stall guard pauses after a stall to give playback time to recover; your own playback controls take priority.

The selected duration is a request. The website, connection, available memory and protected-stream restrictions determine how much can actually be buffered.

## Episode tracking

AniBrave on Android and the desktop extension can remember viewing progress on supported sites, including Crunchyroll, HiAnime and AniKoto.

- Progress starts counting after real playback, rather than a page simply being open. Pausing, seeking and advertisements do not count as watched coverage.
- Episodes reach watched status at approximately **85% coverage**. Rewatching an earlier section preserves previous progress.
- Continue watching can reopen a known episode link and restore its position and speed when the player supports it.
- If an episode cannot be identified confidently, you can confirm it or enter it manually.
- Edit or delete entries in the viewing archive, mark an episode done, or dismiss a continuation suggestion without deleting its history.

Viewing history is stored locally and excluded from private browsing. Android and desktop maintain separate histories; there is no automatic cross-device synchronization. Restoring progress depends on the site making the matching video available and seekable.

## AniBrave desktop extension

The extension adds playback-speed controls, site-view choices and episode tracking to Brave on desktop.

Playback speeds range from **0.25× to 5×** in quarter-speed steps. Supported website controls and the extension reflect one another’s changes, while the extension can request speeds beyond those listed by the site. Player restrictions may still apply.

Choose normal desktop browsing, a phone-sized mobile view, or a mobile identity that retains the current window width. Site preferences are remembered. Mobile emulation may show Brave’s debugger notice; opening developer tools can interrupt it, in which case select the mode again. Some websites require a reload before changing layouts.

### Install the extension

1. Extract **AniBrave-Extension-1.0.0.zip** into a permanent folder.
2. Open `brave://extensions` and enable **Developer mode**.
3. Select **Load unpacked** and choose the extracted extension folder.
4. Pin the extension if you want quick access to its controls.

To update an existing installation, replace the files in its existing folder, press **Reload** on the extension’s card, then reload affected website tabs. Keeping the existing installation preserves its settings.

## Banners

The banner collection includes **177 app banners**. Browse and preview available artwork in AniHome, or use the optional banner download for offline access. You can always choose a personal image or keep an app icon instead.

## Install or update the Android apps

1. Download the APK for the app you want.
2. Open it on your Android device and allow installation from that source when prompted.
3. For AniHome, select it as the default Home app when you are ready to use it as your launcher.
4. Enable the optional permissions for features you want to use.

Install an update over the existing app to retain its data. Uninstalling or clearing app storage removes local settings and data.

## Permissions and privacy

AniHome requests optional access according to the features you enable:

- **Notification access** shows and manages notifications in the sidebar.
- **Accessibility access** supports actions such as locking the screen and opening Recents.
- **System-settings access** allows rotation changes.
- **File and media access** supports recent files and choosing artwork.
- **Shizuku access**, where supported, provides additional system controls.

Home layouts, quick notes and episode history are stored locally. Opening websites, searching the web and fetching banner artwork use a network connection. Websites retain their own account, privacy and content policies.

## Things to know

- Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, widgets, navigation and rotation behavior can vary with Android version and device firmware.
- Subscription requirements, protected players and website restrictions still apply. Playback speed, buffering and progress restoration may not be available on every site.
- The Android browser uses Brave **1.98.60**. This launch does not include a newer upstream browser engine.
